Privacy Policy

PullRows is built to keep your data in your browser. This policy explains what stays local, what leaves only when you turn a feature on, and where it goes.

1. Summary

By default, nothing you scrape leaves your browser. Scraped data, the Data Table, history and recipes are stored on your device. The extension has no analytics, telemetry or ads, and we don't sell or share your data.

Data is sent elsewhere only when you choose a feature that needs it: AI features (to Anthropic, with your own key), the Claude connector (through our pass-through relay to your Claude account) and licence checks (to Dodo Payments).

2. Who we are

“PullRows”, “we” and “us” refer to the maker of the PullRows browser extension and this website. For anything in this policy, email support@pullrows.com.

3. Data that stays on your device

PullRows does its work inside your browser. The following is stored locally using Chrome's extension storage (chrome.storage) on your device, and is not sent to us:

  • Scraped data — the content you choose to extract from web pages, and the rows in your Data Table.
  • History — your last 30 results.
  • Recipes — the scraping setups you save, including any start page URL.
  • Settings — your preferences, your Anthropic API key if you add one, your Claude connector settings and private link, and your licence key.

When PullRows fetches pages for you (for example in Page Extractor's Fast mode or the Email Finder), your browser requests those pages directly from the websites, with your cookies, as if you had opened them. Those websites can see your IP address and requests, as with normal browsing.

4. What we never do

  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels or crash reporting in the extension.
  • No advertising, and no use of your data for advertising.
  • No selling, renting or trading of your data.
  • No use of your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • No reading of your scraped data by people — except content you choose to send us, for example in a support email.

5. Optional features that send data

Each of these is off until you turn it on or use it.

a. AI features (AI Extract, AI column, Ask Claude)

When you run an AI feature, the extension sends your instruction together with the page text, URLs or table rows you selected to Anthropic's API (api.anthropic.com), using your own API key. The request goes directly from your browser to Anthropic; it does not pass through our servers, and we never see your key. Anthropic processes this data under the terms and privacy policy that apply to your Anthropic API account.

b. Claude connector (MCP)

When you switch on the connector, the extension keeps a connection open to the PullRows relay server, which we operate. When you ask Claude to use PullRows:

  • Claude sends tool calls to the relay — for example a URL to open, a selector or an instruction.
  • Your browser runs the tool and sends the results back through the relay to your Claude account. Results can include page content, extracted rows, tab titles and URLs, and screenshots of a tab.

The relay is a pass-through. Tool calls and results are held in memory only for as long as it takes to deliver them, and are not stored or logged. We keep limited operational metadata — such as timestamps, the name of the tool called, response sizes, error codes and connecting IP addresses — to run, secure and rate-limit the service. This metadata does not include page content and is deleted within 30 days.

Once results reach Claude, Anthropic processes them under the agreement that applies to your Claude account. Your private link contains a secret that identifies your browser; anyone with it could send tool calls to your browser, so keep it private. You can revoke it at any time with Generate new link. Sensitive sites — webmail and account pages, banking and payment sites, password managers, browser pages and local network addresses — are blocked for connector tools, and you can add more in Settings.

c. Licence validation

If you buy Pro or Lifetime and enter a licence key, the extension sends the licence key and a randomly generated instance ID for that browser to Dodo Payments to activate the licence on that browser and to re-validate it about once a day. No scraped data, browsing history or page content is sent.

6. Payments and licences

Purchases are processed by Dodo Payments, which acts as the merchant of record. Dodo Payments collects your name, email address, billing country or address and payment details, and handles tax. We never see your full card number. We receive order details — your email address, country, product, price and licence key — so we can deliver your licence, provide support and handle refunds. Dodo Payments' own privacy policy applies to the data it collects.

7. This website

  • pullrows.com is a static site. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts.
  • Our hosting provider processes standard request logs (such as IP address, user agent and requested page) to deliver and protect the site.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
  • If you switch the pricing currency, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.
  • The optional feedback form on our uninstall page only opens your email app. Nothing is sent unless you send that email.

9. Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We use data only to provide and improve PullRows' single purpose — extracting web data into tables that you control — and only as described in this policy. We do not transfer it to third parties except as needed to provide features you choose (as described in section 5), to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.

10. Retention and deletion

  • Local data stays until you delete it. Clear rows and sheets in the Data Table, delete recipes, or clear History in the History view. Uninstalling PullRows removes all of its local storage from your browser.
  • Connector traffic is not stored. Operational metadata is deleted within 30 days.
  • Licence and order records are kept for as long as your licence is active and afterwards as required for tax and accounting.
  • Support emails are kept for as long as needed to help you, and deleted on request where the law allows.

11. Security

All connections to the relay, Anthropic and Dodo Payments use HTTPS. The connector is off by default, uses a secret link you can revoke, is read-only until you allow actions and downloads, and blocks sensitive sites. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your connector link and licence key private.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under the EU and UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and US state laws such as the CCPA — you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because most PullRows data lives only on your device, you can exercise many of these rights directly in the extension. For anything we hold, such as order records or support emails, email support@pullrows.com. We'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Our relay, website host and payment provider may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

13. Children

PullRows is not directed at children. It is not intended for anyone under 13, or under 18 where local law requires parental consent to process a child's data (including India). We do not knowingly collect children's personal data; if you believe we have, contact us and we'll delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. If a change materially affects how data is handled — for example, a new kind of data leaving your device — we'll announce it in the changelog and in the extension before it takes effect.

15. Contact

Questions, requests or concerns: support@pullrows.com.

Changelog

What's new

Every release of PullRows, newest first. Chrome updates the extension automatically.

v1.4.0

October 26, 2026

New

Watch pages, see scraping in the chat, sync to Sheets

  • Monitors: re-run any saved recipe every hour, day or week in the background and get a Chrome alert on what changed — new rows, rows that are gone, changed values and price drops (with the % change). Every run is saved in History with a “Changes” sheet. Free: 1 daily monitor; Pro: unlimited, hourly.
  • Screenshots in the chat: page tools used through the Claude connector now return a small screenshot, so you see what was scraped right in Claude — on desktop or your phone.
  • Ask Claude to watch a page: new monitor_create and monitors_list tools — “tell me when anything on this page gets cheaper”.
  • Google Sheets: send the Data Table to your own Google Sheet in one click (one-minute setup, no Google sign-in in the extension). Pro auto-syncs monitor changes.
  • AI enrichment (Pro): add industry, company size, B2B/B2C and a 0–100 lead score with the reason, or sentiment, summaries, name splitting and translation — one click on any table.

v1.3.0

October 18, 2026

Hardened

Full audit and live testing on real sites

  • Pagination: auto-detected “Next” buttons get a selector that matches only that button and are found again on every page (some sites made PullRows click the logo instead).
  • Tables: two-level headers with rowspan/colspan (Wikipedia) name columns like “City proper · Population”; footnotes and hidden styles are ignored.
  • Full titles instead of “A Light in the …” when the site keeps the full text in a tooltip.
  • Social & email finder reads JavaScript-built sites (and sites that refuse background requests) in a hidden tab.
  • Phones: unbroken numbers like +91 9876543210 are found; order numbers are not mistaken for phones.
  • YouTube: exact views and likes on Shorts; Amazon: clean /dp/ links and a sponsored flag; Google Maps: website and opening hours fixes.
  • Claude connector safety: safety rules now also apply after redirects and to links found inside pages; trailing-dot and IPv6 tricks blocked; buttons chosen by Claude are clicked only with “Allow actions”; long jobs return their rows before Claude’s 60-second limit.
  • Your table is safe (1.3.1): when Claude's results arrive, your own Data Table — including hand edits — is saved in full to History first.
  • Safety at read time (1.3.1): a page that redirects itself to a blocked site is caught at the moment it is read, and its data is never returned.
  • Speed and robustness: huge pages are scanned in milliseconds; a broken mailto/tel link no longer stops a scan; cancelled Claude jobs free their slot at once.

v1.2.0

October 18, 2026

New

X and Reddit

  • Reddit in Trend Finder: a topic or r/subreddit is read entirely in the background from Reddit's own feed: title and text, upvotes, comments, crossposts, subreddit, author, age, video length. Ranked by upvotes per hour and discussion per upvote; the Patterns sheet adds the communities and formats the winners share.
  • X (Twitter) in Trend Finder: search results and timelines with text, hashtags, views, likes, reposts, replies, bookmarks, media type and time. X only shows posts to logged-in users, so PullRows opens the search in a tab and scrolls it.
  • post_details and the side panel read single X and Reddit posts too, and the List Extractor has an X posts preset (counts as numbers, ads skipped).

v1.1.0

October 18, 2026

New

Trend Finder, background-first scraping

  • Trend Finder: enter a topic or #hashtag (or open a search/profile page, or paste links) and get the best Reels, TikToks and YouTube Shorts ranked by momentum, engagement and reach, with caption, hook, hashtags, views, likes, comments, shares, saves, sound, length, upload time and creator followers. A Patterns sheet shows what the winners share. Pro adds the spoken script where the platform publishes captions, and an optional AI breakdown with new post ideas.
  • Background first: Page Extractor's new Auto mode reads pages without opening anything and opens a muted hidden tab only for pages that need JavaScript or a login.
  • CAPTCHA and login walls: list runs pause, bring the tab forward and carry on once you've solved it, instead of collecting empty pages.
  • Clean numbers: “Convert to numbers” turns ₹1,299, 1.2K, 3,4 Mio and “4.5 out of 5” into real numbers (exported to Excel as numbers), and “Remove tracking from links” strips utm_, fbclid and Amazon ref codes.
  • List chooser: every list found on the page is in a menu, plus a “Next suggestion” button.
  • Claude connector: new trends_find and post_details tools.

v1.0.1

October 17, 2026

Improved

Works on more kinds of websites

  • Tables: column names come from the table's headers, including Wikipedia-style header rows and old sites that use bold cells; the header row is no longer extracted as data. Grids built from divs (role="grid") work the same way.
  • Two-row items: lists where each item spans two rows (Hacker News, many forums) and term/definition lists come out as one row per item.
  • Right value in every column: fixed a case where a column could read a neighbouring element on sites with generated class names (search results, Hacker News).
  • Menus and footers: navigation, mega-menus, sidebars and footers no longer win over the real list.
  • Single-page apps: Auto-detect waits a few seconds for lists that appear after the page loads.
  • “Next” in 35+ languages and “Load more” in 20+, including Hindi, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Arabic, Russian, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese, plus wordings like “Show 20 more”.
  • More data per item: background images, star ratings in image alt text, data-* attributes (SKU, IDs) and web-component attributes, and constant values such as a shared location are now kept.
  • Reddit preset for the new and old Reddit layouts.
  • Clearer errors on pages Chrome won't let extensions read (PDF viewer, error pages, local files).

v1.0.0

October 16, 2026

Launch

Hello, PullRows

The extension formerly known as “Web Scraper Extension” launches as PullRows.

  • Redesigned side panel — new name, icon and look; clearer tool tabs, status and progress while a job runs.
  • Pro plan — monthly, yearly and a launch Lifetime licence. The free plan keeps every tool and unlimited CSV export. Paste your licence key in Settings → Plan; one key works on up to 3 browsers.
  • Claude connector safety controls — off by default; a private link you can regenerate to revoke access; read-only by default, with separate switches for actions (click, type) and downloads; webmail, banking, password-manager, browser and local-network pages blocked.
  • Security hardening — stricter validation of messages between the side panel, pages and the relay, and tighter limits on the relay.

How it works

Watch a page turn into rows.

No selectors, no code, no account. Scroll through what PullRows does on every page, in about three clicks.

  1. 01

    Open any page with a list

    Search results, a directory, a shop category, a job board — including sites you're logged in to. Then open PullRows from the toolbar.

  2. 02

    Auto-detect finds every item

    Click one item, or press Auto-detect. PullRows scores every repeating block on the page and outlines the list you mean — then names the columns from the content.

  3. 03

    Every item becomes a row

    Titles, prices, ratings and links land in a clean table. Next buttons, “Load more” and infinite scroll keep adding rows until the list ends.

  4. 04

    Export — or hand it to Claude

    Download CSV for free, or Excel and JSON with Pro. Or ask Claude to filter, enrich and summarise the table for you.

    Eleven tools, one table

    Every scraping job you actually do, in one side panel.

    Each tool writes into the same editable Data Table, so you can mix results, dedupe, clean and export in one place.

    • List Extractor

      Turn any list, grid or table into rows with readable columns. Follows pagination for you.

    • Page Extractor

      Define fields once, then pull them from hundreds of URLs. One row per page.

    • Email Finder

      Crawls contact, about and team pages. Decodes protected and “name (at) domain” addresses.

    • Social Links

      LinkedIn, X, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and GitHub profiles for a list of sites. Skips share buttons.

    • Image Downloader

      Full-size originals from srcset, lazy loads and CSS backgrounds, saved with readable names.

    • Shopify Exporter

      A store's whole catalogue — per variant, per product, or ready-to-import Shopify CSV.

    • Sitemap Explorer

      Every URL from robots.txt, sitemap indexes and .gz files, grouped by section and filterable.

    • Page → Markdown

      Clean reader-mode text with title, author and date. Copy it or save it as .md.

    • Quick Scan

      One click for emails, phones, links, tables, prices and socials — one sheet each.

    • your API key

      AI Extract · AI column · Ask Claude

      Describe the table you want in plain English. Fill a new column from every row (“classify the industry”). Ask questions and get answers with numbers from your data.

    Pro-grade scraping

    Built for the messy, modern web.

    Sites lazy-load, virtualise and obfuscate. PullRows was built against exactly that, so your export matches what you saw.

    • Scrolls one screen at a timeCatches lazy-loaded and virtualised items that a jump-to-bottom scraper misses.

    • Merges half-loaded rowsWhen an item renders in stages, the pieces are joined into one complete row.
    • Readable column namestitle, price, rating, date — named from the content, even on sites with scrambled class names.
    • Runs with your loginsIt's your browser, so member areas and dashboards work. PullRows never sees your passwords.

    Claude connector · MCP

    Your browser. Your logins. Driven by Claude.

    Turn on the connector and Claude can open pages, extract lists, crawl for emails and read your Data Table — inside your own Chrome, with the sessions you're already signed in to. No cloud browsers. No credits.

    1. 1
      Turn it on in SettingsSide panel → Settings → Claude connector. It's off until you switch it on.
    2. 2
      Copy your private linkIt's the only key to your browser. Generate a new one any time to revoke the old.
    3. 3
      Add it as a custom connector in ClaudeSettings → Connectors → Add custom connector, then paste the link.
    $claude mcp add --transport http pullrows <your-link>

Refund Policy

14 days, no questions asked. If PullRows isn't right for you, you get your money back.

Effective October 16, 2026

The short version: email us within 14 days of being charged and we'll refund that payment in full. You don't need to give a reason.

What's covered

  • Pro monthly and yearly — any payment, including a renewal, within 14 days of the charge.
  • Lifetime — within 14 days of purchase.

The free plan is free, so there's nothing to refund. Try it first — every tool is included.

How to get a refund

  1. Email support@pullrows.com from the address you used at checkout, or include your order ID from the receipt.
  2. We'll confirm within 2 business days and issue the refund through Dodo Payments, our merchant of record.
  3. The money goes back to your original payment method. Banks usually take 5–10 business days to show it.

When a refund is issued, the licence key for that purchase is deactivated and PullRows returns to the free plan. Your local data — Data Table, history and recipes — isn't affected.

Cancelling a subscription

Cancelling stops future renewals. You can cancel any time from the link in your receipt email or by emailing us. If you cancel more than 14 days after a charge, Pro stays active until the end of that period and you won't be charged again.

Fair use

The guarantee is meant for people trying PullRows in good faith. We may decline repeated refund requests for the same product from the same person. If something's wrong with a charge, please email us before opening a dispute with your bank — it's faster for you.

Your legal rights

This policy adds to, and doesn't limit, any rights you have under consumer law where you live.
